# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + React 18, React Router 6, Vanilla CSS design system (`client/src/styles/shared.css`), Express backend & CRM (`server/`).

## Users

Brand directors, exhibition managers, corporate event organizers, retail rollout leads, and commercial fitout project heads looking for a high-craft physical production and execution partner.

## Product Purpose

EGS is a premium production house and craft partner specializing in exhibitions, events, interior fitouts, and retail brand environments. Its digital platform presents an image-led, high-authority brand showcase for clients while housing an integrated administrative CRM and project operations suite.

## Positioning

"A production house with memory." Unlike generic fabrication vendors or transactional event agencies, EGS improves through repetition—maintaining project dossiers, crew continuity, post-event debriefs, and carry-forward design/fabrication logic so every repeat build is faster, smoother, and more refined.

## Operating Context

Multi-phase physical production workflows: concept & spatial design, engineering & site surveys, custom joinery/fabrication, on-site load-in & build, live event support, load-out, and post-project debrief dossiers. Evaluated on reliability, aesthetic finish, operational calm, and commercial discipline.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Public Brand Showcase**: Vite + React frontend rendering distinct, image-led service surfaces for Exhibitions, Events, Fitouts, and Retail (`client/src/pages/`).
- **Admin CRM & Operations**: Built-in CRM workspace (`client/src/admin/crm/`) and backend services (`server/`) handling email templates, client pipelines, and quote requests.
- **Visual Constraints**: Tactile paper backgrounds, editorial serif typography (Instrument Serif), dark ink text, quiet authority, zero generic agency buzzwords, strict alignment with `shared.css`.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name**: EGS
- **Voice**: Literary precision, quiet confidence, specificity over hype, concrete operational terminology (dossier, crew, cue sheet, shell, foreman, load-out, debrief).
- **Signature Promise**: "We remember every project."

## Evidence on Hand

- Source-of-truth positioning and copy guidelines in `EGS_WEBSITE_CONTENT_BIBLE.md`.
- Visual system and interaction direction in `DESIGN_PHILOSOPHY.md`.
- Shared design system tokens in `client/src/styles/shared.css`.
- Page-level composition specs in `content/page-specs/`.

## Product Principles

1. **Operationally Calm, Aesthetically Refined**: Invisible operational discipline enables emotional and visual excellence.
2. **Carry-Forward Continuity**: Treat debriefs and project dossiers as part of the core product; repeat engagements build upon past learning.
3. **Front-of-Stage Craft**: Turn backend craftsmanship (joinery precision, cue-sheet timing, site safety) into visible value for the client.
4. **Specificity Over Hype**: Avoid corporate buzzwords; rely on concrete metrics, named processes, and tactile visual proof.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Responsive web layouts, semantic HTML5 hierarchy, contrast-compliant typography (`--ink` on `--paper`), accessible interactive controls, and clear focus states.
