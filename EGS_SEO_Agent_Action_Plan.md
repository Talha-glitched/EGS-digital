# EGS SEO Agent Action Plan

## Mission
Improve the organic search visibility and qualified lead generation of `www.exhibitgraphicsign.com` in Dubai/UAE.

Primary commercial theme: **exhibition stand contractor / builder / company in Dubai**.

Secondary authority themes:
- exhibition stand design and fabrication
- Dubai/UAE exhibition venues
- major exhibitions and event-specific searches
- graduation ceremony production
- retail branding and rollouts
- signage
- commercial fit-outs

## Operating Rules
1. Never invent clients, projects, awards, certifications, statistics, venue relationships, reviews, backlinks, or business claims.
2. Use first-party EGS information for factual claims about EGS.
3. Do not copy competitor wording.
4. Do not create thin doorway/location pages.
5. Do not keyword-stuff.
6. Do not publish AI-generated content without factual verification and substantive editing.
7. Every page must have a defined search intent and primary keyword.
8. Prioritize commercial intent and genuine project evidence.
9. Preserve existing URLs unless there is a clear SEO reason to change them; use 301 redirects when URLs must change.
10. Do not fabricate schema data.
11. Do not create fake reviews or manipulate review text.
12. Do not buy bulk backlinks, PBN links, spam directory links or irrelevant links.
